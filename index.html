01 — The house

An evening that
refuses to end

Behind the yellow façade on Kote Apkhazi is a room where the same thing has happened for more than ten years: the grill glows from midday, the pots have been on a low flame since morning, and at some point in the evening somebody starts to sing.

Our kitchen came from Isfahan and Shiraz — and picked up whatever it liked on the way to Tbilisi: the sumac of the Levant, the baharat of the Gulf, Georgian bread from the clay oven. What stays is the Persian rule: no spice pushes to the front, everything is given time.

02 — From the kitchen

Four to begin with

If this is your first time here, order these. Everything else is on the menu.

Kabāb-e Barg

کباب برگ

Beef fillet in thin leaves, marinated overnight in onion juice and saffron, brushed with saffron butter.

Ghormeh Sabzi

قورمه‌سبزی

The national dish: seven herbs, kidney beans and lamb, sharpened with dried lime.

Māhiche

ماهیچه

Lamb shank braised four hours in saffron and onion, until it slides off the bone.

Kashk-e Bademjān

کشک بادمجان

Aubergines charred over open flame and crushed to a cream, with fermented whey, mint oil, garlic and fried onions.
