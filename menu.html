01

Starters & Salads

A Persian table starts wide: small bowls, a heap of fresh herbs, bread straight from the stone oven. All of it meant to be shared.

Pišġazā

Kashk-e Bademjān

کشک بادمجان

Aubergines charred over open flame and crushed to a cream, with fermented whey, mint oil, garlic and fried onions.

Aubergine Rolls with Walnut

رول بادمجان با گردو

Thin slices of aubergine rolled around a walnut and garlic cream, finished with pomegranate seeds and herbs.

Lentil Soup

سوپ عدس

Smooth red lentils with turmeric, cumin and a generous squeeze of lemon. Served hot, with flatbread.

Salād-e Shirāzi

سالاد شیرازی

Cucumber, tomato and onion in a fine dice, dressed with lime juice, olive oil and dried mint.

Fattoush

فتوش

A crisp Levantine salad with sumac, pomegranate molasses and toasted flatbread.

Greek Salad

سالاد یونانی

Tomato, cucumber, pepper and olives with feta, oregano and cold-pressed olive oil.

02

From the Charcoal Grill

Everything straight over glowing charcoal, nothing in between. Every skewer comes with saffron rice or flatbread, a grilled tomato, basil and sumac.

Kabāb

Kabāb-e Barg

کباب برگ

Beef fillet in thin leaves, marinated overnight in onion juice and saffron, brushed with saffron butter.

Kabāb-e Soltāni

کباب سلطانی

The sultan of skewers: one Barg and one Koobideh side by side, on a bed of saffron rice.

Kabāb-e Koobideh (Lula)

کباب کوبیده

Lamb and beef twice minced with onion and sumac, pressed onto the flat skewer by hand.

Kabāb-e Bakhtiāri

کباب بختیاری

The skewer of the mountain nomads: saffron-marinated chicken and fillet alternating down the spit.

Shishlik

شیشلیک

Lamb chops in the Shandiz manner, marinated for twenty-four hours and grilled exactly to the point.

Kabāb-e Filet

کباب فیله

Pure fillet cubes on the skewer — salt, saffron and butter, and nothing else.

Joojeh Kabāb on the Bone

جوجه کباب با استخوان

Chicken on the bone marinated in saffron, yoghurt and lime — crisp outside, still juicy within.

Half Grilled Chicken

نیم مرغ کبابی

Cooked slowly over the embers, with saffron rice, grilled vegetables and garlic sauce.

03

Khoresht — Persian Stews

Hours on a low flame. Every khoresht arrives with chelo — steamed long-grain rice under a golden tahdig crust.

Khoresht

Ghormeh Sabzi

قورمه‌سبزی

The national dish: seven herbs, kidney beans and lamb, sharpened with dried lime.

Gheimeh

قیمه

Yellow split peas and lamb in tomato and Limu Omani, crowned with a nest of fried potato straw.

Māhiche

ماهیچه

Lamb shank braised four hours in saffron and onion, until it slides off the bone.

Khoresht Selection for Two

بشقاب خورش برای دو نفر

Three stews from the day’s pots, with rice, bread and the sides of the house. For two.

04

Rice & Feast Dishes

Rice is not a side here. It is washed, soaked, boiled briefly and then steamed — until every grain stands on its own.

Polo

Chicken Biryāni

بریانی مرغ

Layered spiced rice with saffron, cardamom and cinnamon, finished in the pot with marinated chicken.

Mazbi Chicken

مرغ مظبی

Chicken cooked over wood on spiced rice, in the manner of the Arabian peninsula — with tomato sauce and sumac.

Machboos

مجبوس

Long-grain rice with baharat, loomi and braised meat — the Gulf classic, meant for the middle of the table.

05

Fish & Seafood

From the Caspian to the Persian Gulf — fish arrives whole on the plate, with lemon and herbs.

Māhi

Grilled Dorado

ماهی دورادو کبابی

A whole dorado over charcoal, stuffed with herbs and garlic, with grilled lemon and saffron rice.

Seafood Platter

بشقاب غذای دریایی

Prawns, squid and the fish of the day, with lemon, herb oil and toasted bread. Made for sharing.

06

Sweets & Tea

The evening ends the way it ought to begin: black tea from the samovar, and something sweet beside it.

Shirini o Chāy

Tea & Baklava

چای و باقلوا

Black tea from the samovar, with baklava of pistachio and rose water.

Baklava Plate

بشقاب باقلوا

Three kinds of baklava — pistachio, walnut and honey — dusted with saffron sugar.

Dessert of the Day

دسر روز

Whatever the kitchen made today: faloodeh, shole zard or saffron ice cream. Just ask.

Berry Sharbat

شربت میوه‌های قرمز

House-made red berry syrup poured over ice, with mint and lime.
